mission

our mission against modern algorithms and ai.

our mission against modern algorithms and ai.

nowNoise exists to push back against ai generated music, algorithm driven discovery, and a culture where taste is increasingly shaped by machines. our mission is to make music feel human again, built around real artists, real scenes, real communities, and the unexpected moments that make discovery matter.

nowNoise puts human taste first. discover artists beyond the algorithm, support original voices, and connect with people who genuinely care about music. no synthetic culture, no feed telling you what to like. just authentic music, shared by real people.
